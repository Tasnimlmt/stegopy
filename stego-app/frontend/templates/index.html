Help & Guide

Step-by-step instructions for hiding, extracting, and using Shamir's Secret Sharing
📥 How to Hide Messages
  1. Click "Hide Message" in navigation
  2. Upload a carrier image (PNG, JPG, BMP)
  3. Enter your secret message (max ~500 chars)
  4. Set a strong password for encryption
  5. Choose a security mode: Standard, Deniable, or Duress
  6. Click "Hide Message in Image" and download the result
🔍 How to Extract Messages
  1. Click "Extract Message"
  2. Upload the encoded image
  3. Enter the correct password
  4. Click "Extract Hidden Message"
  5. Copy or download the revealed message

🔀 Shamir's Secret Sharing (SSS)
Split a secret into N shares where any K shares can reconstruct the original. To split: set N and K, upload exactly N carrier images, and click split. To reconstruct: upload at least K share images and provide the decryption password.
💡 Example: N=5, K=3 means create 5 shares, and any 3 can reconstruct the secret. Distribute shares to trusted parties.
Security Best Practices: Use strong, unique passwords (12+ chars), store shares separately, and keep at least K shares safe.
